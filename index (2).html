01 — About

Designing with purpose.
Building with precision.

SSM
Est. Portfolio — 2026

I believe architecture is more than creating beautiful spaces — it's about solving real problems through thoughtful design, technical precision, and innovation. Every line drawn, every detail developed, and every model created should contribute to environments that are functional, sustainable, and built to last.

I'm Sergio Simon Manyonyi, an Architectural Technologist driven by a passion for transforming concepts into buildable realities. My work sits at the intersection of architectural design, Building Information Modeling, and construction technology, where creativity meets technical execution.

"Architecture should inspire through design, perform through technology, and endure through thoughtful construction."

My design process begins with understanding people, place, and purpose. Through research, site analysis, and iterative development, I create solutions that respond to both human needs and construction realities — from early sketches to detailed construction drawings and immersive visualizations.
